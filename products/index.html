AlephTX

AlephTX combines recorded order-book depth, funding, and independent reference prices to study relative value across venues.

Build the dataset
Align market depth, funding events, and reference prices.
Replay execution
Model order latency and partial fills across both legs.
Read the costs
Inspect simulated fills, fees, slippage, and settled funding.

Evidence and risk

Oasis Agent

Oasis Agent records the inputs behind a strategy decision so researchers can replay it and examine the execution limits separately.

Keep the evidence
Preserve the original observation, its source, and the state it came from.
Replay the decision
Revisit the decision and its accounting in simulation.
Check the limits
Evaluate exposure, loss limits, and authorization before execution.
