Markets2026-11-12

Compare funding on the same clock

Start a cross-venue funding study with settlement timestamps and a common holding window. A quoted rate may use a different time basis than the payment itself. Reconstruct cash flows over the same window before ranking spreads, then include fees and slippage.

What to watch An apparent spread can disappear when settlement times differ or the available size cannot be filled at the assumed price.

Robotics data2026-11-12

Validate robot data at episode boundaries

Use an episode as the unit for your first dataset quality check. On a small sample, resolve video, state, and action ranges through the metadata. Inspect timestamps and alignment before paying the cost of a full dataset pass.

What to watch A sample can miss corruption that only appears across files, cameras, or recording sessions. Expand coverage once the checks pass.
