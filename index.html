Research notes

Research methods2026-11-12

Replay against the order book

A mid-price spread can suggest a hypothesis. To test execution, replay both legs against recorded depth and account for latency, partial fills, fees, and funding. Keep the two fill histories separate so the cost of each leg remains visible.

What to watch A replay models what displayed liquidity might fill. Live orders can miss that liquidity or fill at a different price.

Market mechanics2026-11-12

Compare funding on the same clock

Start a cross-venue funding study with settlement timestamps and a common holding window. A quoted rate may use a different time basis than the payment itself. Reconstruct cash flows over the same window before ranking spreads, then include fees and slippage.

What to watch An apparent spread can disappear when settlement times differ or the available size cannot be filled at the assumed price.
