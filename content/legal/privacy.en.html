Visiting the site

AlphaWink publishes public research and project information at alphawink.com. Our site scripts handle navigation and display in your browser. GitHub Pages hosts the site and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

Email correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond and follow up on your enquiry. Email service providers process correspondence to deliver and store it. We keep correspondence for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and meet applicable obligations.

External services

Links to repositories, documentation and sources take you to services with their own privacy practices.
